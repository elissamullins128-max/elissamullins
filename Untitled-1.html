Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

I believe in transparency and clarity. Here are answers to some of the questions I'm asked most often by prospective author clients.

What types of authors do you work with? +

I work with both traditionally published and self-published authors across all genres. Whether you're launching your debut novel, your tenth book, or transitioning from traditional to indie publishing, I can help. I'm most effective with authors who are serious about their careers and ready to invest strategically in their growth.

How early should I start marketing my book? +

Ideally, 3-6 months before your launch date. The pre-launch phase is critical for building anticipation, securing early reviews, growing your email list, and establishing the foundation for a strong launch week. However, it's never too late — I also work with authors on backlist revitalization and ongoing marketing for previously published books.

Do you guarantee bestseller status? +

No ethical marketer can guarantee bestseller status — there are too many variables at play. What I do guarantee is a strategic, well-executed campaign built on proven methodologies. My track record speaks for itself: over 50 authors I've worked with have achieved bestseller status in their categories.

Can you help with self-published books? +

Absolutely. A significant portion of my clients are self-published authors. In many ways, marketing is even more critical for indie authors since they don't have the built-in infrastructure of a traditional publisher. I'll help you compete — and win — in any market.

What makes you different from other book marketers? +

Three things: depth of publishing knowledge, commitment to bespoke strategy, and an unwavering focus on measurable results. I don't use templates or cookie-cutter approaches. Every campaign is built from scratch based on deep research into your book, genre, audience, and competitive landscape.

How do I get started working with you? +

Simply fill out the contact form below or email me directly. I'll respond within 24 hours to discuss your book, your goals, and whether we're a good fit to work together. There's no pressure and no obligation — just an honest conversation about how I might be able to help.
